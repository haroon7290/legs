# Homepage image prompts — Legs Relief

Paste-ready prompts for every image on the homepage, written to match the brand reference
photo: a bright, airy bedroom, natural window light, beige linen bedding with navy and
mustard accents, and the black criss-cross elastic wrap.

**Before you start**
- **Attach the reference.** Attach your product photo (the black criss-cross wrap on the foot) as a
  reference image (ChatGPT/Gemini: attach it; Midjourney: image prompt or `--oref`). It is the
  best way to keep the wrap identical in every image.
- **Product photos should be real.** For the product gallery, AI images are placeholders only.
  Swap in photos of the product you actually ship.
- **Text comes out garbled.** Every prompt says "no text". Add headlines and logos afterwards in Canva.
  Your reference photo has its headline baked in, which is great for ads. For the website, use the
  no-text versions below, because the theme puts the text next to the image.
- **Export sizes** are listed per image. Upload JPG or WebP, under 1 MB each.

**The product, described.** This block is already inside every prompt below; you can also reuse it for new images:

> a black woven-elastic foot wrap, about 5 cm (2 in) wide, that stays on the middle of the foot
> only: it loops under the arch and crosses in an X on top of the foot, with a small diamond-shaped
> opening where the bands cross. It does not go around the ankle: the ankle bone, the back of the
> heel and the Achilles tendon are bare. Toes and heel are left uncovered. Matte black with a fine
> ribbed elastic texture, flat and low-profile.

Keep the "does not go around the ankle" sentence. Without it, image AIs tend to draw an ankle
brace with a strap looping behind the heel, because that's the wrap they've seen most.

**Negative prompt** (if your tool supports one):

```
text, letters, watermark, logo, extra toes, six toes, deformed feet, extra limbs, shoe, sock, sandal, buckle, velcro logo, ankle brace, ankle strap, strap behind the heel, strap around the Achilles tendon, sports tape, kinesiology tape, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, oversaturated, HDR
```

## 1. Hero (top of page)

- **Size:** 1600 × 2000 px (4:5). Keep the foot in the middle third; phones crop it to 5:4.
- **Upload:** Theme editor › Hero › Image

```
Photorealistic lifestyle photograph in a bright, airy minimalist bedroom. A woman's bare foot rests on the edge of a bed in side profile, toes pointing left, her lower leg rising diagonally out of frame to the right. The foot wears a black woven-elastic foot wrap, about 2 inches wide, staying on the middle of the foot only: looping under the arch and crossing in an X on top of the foot with a small diamond-shaped opening where the bands cross; it does not go around the ankle, so the ankle, the back of the heel and the Achilles tendon are bare; toes and heel uncovered; matte black with a fine ribbed texture. The bed has a soft oatmeal-beige linen duvet in the foreground; behind it, out of focus, navy blue pillows, a mustard-orange cushion and a mustard knit throw. White walls, soft natural daylight from a large window on the left. Camera low, at mattress height, 50mm lens, f/2, shallow depth of field, clean and calm, editorial sleep-brand photography. No text, no logos. --ar 4:5
```

## 2. "It's 2am and your legs won't quit" (problem section)

- **Size:** 1600 × 1600 px (1:1)
- **Upload:** Theme editor › Image with text › Image

```
Photorealistic night-time photograph of a bedroom at 2am. On the bedside table a digital alarm clock glows orange, showing 2:14, next to a half-empty glass of water. Out of focus behind it, a rumpled bed with navy sheets and a mustard throw, the duvet kicked aside and one bare foot hanging restlessly off the edge of the mattress. Lit only by the clock's glow and faint cool moonlight from a window. Restless, sleepless mood. 35mm lens, low light, natural grain, realistic. Colours: deep navy, blue-black shadows, warm orange glow. No faces, no text except the clock digits. --ar 1:1
```

> AI often scrambles clock digits. If it does, crop the clock out or fix the digits in an editor.
