What exactly is Legs Night?

Legs Night is a deep-pressure foot wrap: a firm, contoured pad held snug under each arch by an adjustable strap. You wear it in bed to help calm restless, fidgety legs so you can fall asleep.

How does it work?

Steady pressure on specific points under the foot gives your nervous system a strong, calm signal to focus on. Many people find that it quiets the urge to move. The same idea is behind a prescription-only device that costs over $200 — we just made it simpler and affordable.

Is it a medical device?

No. Legs Night is a comfort product, not a medical device, and it is not FDA-cleared. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. If you have a medical condition, talk to your doctor before use.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Many customers feel calmer legs the first night. For others it takes a week or two of getting the fit right. That's why we ask you to try it for at least 30 nights.

Which size should I choose?

S/M fits US women's 5–9 / men's 4–8. L/XL fits US women's 9.5–13 / men's 8.5–14. Between sizes? Go up — the strap tightens down easily.

Do I wear one or two?

Each order is a pair — one for each foot. Most people wear both, but you can wear just one on the leg that bothers you most.

Is it comfortable to sleep in?

Yes. The outer is a soft, breathable knit and the pad is contoured to the arch. Tighten until the pressure feels firm but comfortable — never painful or tingling.

How do I wash it?

Remove the pad, then hand or machine wash the wrap on cold and let it air dry.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping is free on every order. We ship within 24 hours, and most orders arrive in 4–8 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email.

What is the 90-night guarantee?

Wear Legs Night consistently for at least 30 nights. If it doesn't help, email support@legsnight.com within 90 nights of delivery for a full refund on one unit. Because it's worn against the skin, you don't need to send it back. See our refund policy for details.
