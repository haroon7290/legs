What exactly is Legs Relief?

Legs Relief is a deep-pressure foot wrap: a firm, contoured pad held snug under each arch by a soft, criss-cross elastic wrap. You wear it in bed to help calm restless, fidgety legs so you can drift off.

Is it a medical device?

No. Legs Relief is a comfort product, not a medical device. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. If you have a medical condition, or you're pregnant, speak to your GP or midwife before use.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Some people feel calmer legs on the first night. For others it takes a week or two to get the fit right, which is why we ask you to try it for at least 30 nights.

Which size should I choose?

S/M fits UK shoe sizes 3–7 (EU 35–40). L/XL fits UK sizes 7½–12 (EU 41–47). Between sizes? Go up — the strap tightens down easily.

Do I wear one or two?

Each order is a pair, one for each foot. Most people wear both, but you can wear just one on the leg that bothers you most.

How do I wash it?

Take out the pad, then hand wash or machine wash the wrap at 30°C and let it air dry. Please don't tumble dry it.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is free and tracked on every UK order. Order by 2pm, Monday to Friday, and we dispatch the same day. Most orders arrive in 2–3 working days, and you'll get a tracking link by email.

What is the 90-night guarantee?

Wear Legs Relief for at least 30 nights. If it doesn't help, email support@legrelief.co.uk within 90 nights of delivery and we'll refund the pair you tried in full — you don't need to send it back. Unopened extra pairs from a bundle can be returned for a full refund too. See our refund policy for details.

Can I return it if I change my mind?

Yes. As well as the 90-night guarantee, UK law gives you 14 days from delivery to change your mind. Email us within that time and we'll explain how to send it back for a full refund.

How do I get the free eBook?

It's added to your basket automatically with any pair, free of charge. The download link arrives by email straight after checkout.
