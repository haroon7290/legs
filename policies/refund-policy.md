# Refund policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

We want Legs Relief to help you sleep, so you get 90 nights to decide, on top of your legal
right to cancel. None of this affects your statutory rights.

## Our 90-night guarantee

Wear Legs Relief every night for at least 30 nights. If your legs aren't calmer, email
**support@legsrelief.co.uk** within 90 nights of delivery with your order number, and we'll
refund the pair you tried in full. You don't need to send it back.

- The guarantee covers one worn pair per customer.
- If you bought a bundle, send back any extra pairs unopened and in their original packaging
  within the same 90 nights, and we'll refund those too.

## Your right to cancel (14 days)

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel your order for any reason within
14 days of receiving it.

1. Email **support@legsrelief.co.uk** with your order number to tell us. We'll reply with the
   returns address.
2. Send the items back within 14 days of telling us, unused and in their original packaging if
   you can.
3. We'll refund you, including the original delivery charge, within 14 days of receiving the
   items or proof that you've sent them.

Unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one, you pay the cost of sending it back. We may
take a deduction from the refund if an item comes back worn or damaged beyond what's needed to
try it, for example by trying it on as you would in a shop. If you'd like to try it for longer,
use the 90-night guarantee instead.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or it isn't what you ordered, email us within 30 days
of delivery with a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full, including any return
postage. After 30 days you're still covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015; see our Terms of
service.

## The eBook

The Calm Night Protocol eBook comes free with every pair. If you bought it on its own and
you're not happy with it, email us within 14 days of buying it and we'll refund you.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. We'll email you when we've sent it, and
your bank may take 3–5 working days to show it.
