## Before you paste

Replace the four bits in square brackets with your own details:

- **[Legal business name]**: your limited company's name, or your own name if you're a sole
  trader.
- **[Business address]**: a UK address where you can receive post. It can be a registered office
  or business address service rather than your home.
- **[Company number]**: limited companies only. Sole traders delete that line.
- **[VAT number]**: only if you're VAT-registered. Otherwise delete that line.

## How to paste

1. Open the file on GitHub so it shows formatted (headings and bold text).
2. Select from *Last updated* down to the end and copy it. Leave out the title at the top:
   Shopify adds the title itself.
3. In Shopify go to *Settings › Policies*, open the policy, paste, and click **Save**.

Pasting the formatted text keeps the headings and lists. If you paste plain text, add the
headings again with the editor's toolbar.

## A note on the law

These policies follow UK consumer and data protection law as it stood on the date at the top
of each one: the Consumer Rights Act 2015, the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, UK GDPR,
the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. They
match how the shop is set up (free UK delivery, 2pm cut-off, 90-night guarantee, Shopify
checkout). If you change how you work, update them to match. They're a solid starting point,
not legal advice; if your business grows or does something unusual, have a solicitor look
them over.
