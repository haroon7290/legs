# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

This policy explains what personal information Legs Relief collects when you visit our website
or buy from us, why we collect it, who we share it with and what your rights are. We follow the
UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Who we are

Legs Relief is a trading name of [Legal business name], [Business address]. We are the
controller of your personal information. If you have any questions about this policy or your
information, email **support@legsrelief.co.uk**.

## What we collect

- **When you place an order:** your name, delivery and billing address, email address, phone
  number if you give it, what you bought and how much you paid. Your card details go straight
  to our payment providers; we never see or store your full card number.
- **When you contact us:** your name, email address, order number and whatever you tell us in
  your message.
- **When you sign up for emails:** your email address and your sign-up choice.
- **When you leave a review:** the name you choose to show, your rating, your review and any
  photos you add.
- **When you browse our website:** your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you
  view, what's in your basket and how you reached us. Some of this is collected with cookies
  (see below).

## How we use it, and our lawful basis

UK law says we must have a lawful basis for each way we use your information. Ours are:

- **To take payment, send your order, give delivery updates and email your eBook link.**
  Basis: contract (we need it to do what you asked).
- **To handle returns, refunds and 90-night guarantee claims.** Basis: contract.
- **To keep tax and accounting records.** Basis: legal obligation.
- **To answer your messages.** Basis: legitimate interests (helping you), or contract if it's
  about an order.
- **To prevent fraud and keep our website secure.** Basis: legitimate interests.
- **To ask you to review your order** (one email, which you can opt out of). Basis: legitimate
  interests.
- **To send you offers and news by email.** Basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **For analytics and advertising cookies.** Basis: consent, given through our cookie banner.

## Health information

Some customers mention their health when they write to us, for example about restless legs.
You never have to. If you do, we only use it to reply to you and to handle your order or
guarantee claim, we never use it for marketing, and we delete it once it's no longer needed.
We rely on your explicit consent for this, and you can withdraw it at any time.

## Who we share it with

We only share what each company needs to do its job, and we never sell your information.

- **Shopify**, which runs our online shop and checkout.
- **Payment providers** (Shopify Payments, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay), to take
  your payment and prevent fraud.
- **Delivery companies**, so they can deliver your parcel and send you updates.
- **Our email provider**, to send and receive emails.
- **Apps we use to run the shop**, such as our review and eBook delivery apps.
- **Analytics and advertising partners** such as Google and Meta, only if you accept those
  cookies.
- **Our accountant, HMRC, the police or regulators**, where the law requires it.

Some of these companies store information outside the UK, for example Shopify in Canada and
the United States. When that happens, your information is protected in the ways UK law
requires, such as UK adequacy regulations or the Information Commissioner's International Data
Transfer Agreement.

## How long we keep it

- **Orders, payments and refunds:** six years after the end of the financial year they belong
  to, because UK tax law requires it.
- **Messages to us:** up to two years after we last hear from you, unless they're about an
  order, in which case we keep them with the order.
- **Email sign-up:** until you unsubscribe. After that we keep your email address on a
  do-not-email list so we don't contact you again.
- **Cookies:** for the time shown in our cookie banner, or until you clear them.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files saved by your browser.

- **Essential cookies** keep your basket, run the checkout, keep the website secure and
  remember your cookie choice. The website can't work without them, so they're always on.
- **Analytics cookies** show us how people use the website so we can improve it. We only use
  them if you say yes.
- **Advertising cookies** help us measure our adverts and show them to people who might be
  interested. We only use them if you say yes.

The first time you visit, our cookie banner asks for your choice. To change it later, clear
the cookies for this website in your browser settings and the banner will ask you again.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct anything that's wrong or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your information;
- ask us to limit how we use it, or object to us using it;
- ask for your information in a format you can move to another company;
- withdraw your consent at any time, for example by unsubscribing from our emails.

To use any of these rights, email **support@legsrelief.co.uk**. It's free, and we'll reply
within one month. We may need to check your identity first. Some rights have limits; for
example, we must keep order records for tax purposes.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put
it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):
ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

## Keeping it safe

Our website and checkout use encrypted connections (HTTPS), and payments are processed by
providers that meet the PCI DSS card security standard. Only people who need your information
to run the shop can see it.

## Children

Our shop is for adults. You must be 18 or over to place an order, and we don't knowingly
collect information about children.
