# Shipping policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

## UK delivery

- **Free tracked delivery** on every order. There's nothing extra to pay at checkout.
- **Order by 2pm (UK time), Monday to Friday**, and we dispatch the same day. Orders placed
  after 2pm, at weekends or on bank holidays go out the next working day.
- Most orders arrive **2–3 working days** after dispatch. We email you a tracking link as soon
  as your order is on its way.
- We deliver to every UK address, including Northern Ireland and the Scottish Highlands and
  islands. These can take 1–2 working days longer.

Delivery times are estimates. Very occasionally, for example around Christmas or during
postal strikes, a parcel can take longer.

## Outside the UK

We don't deliver outside the UK yet.

## If nobody's in

The delivery company will leave a card or send you a message telling you how to collect your
parcel or rearrange delivery.

## Late, missing or damaged parcels

If your order hasn't arrived within 7 working days of dispatch, or arrives damaged, email
**support@legsrelief.co.uk** with your order number. We'll track it down or send a replacement
at no cost to you.

Please check your delivery address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away
and we'll do our best to change it before your order is dispatched.

## The eBook

The Calm Night Protocol eBook isn't posted. We email you the download link straight after
checkout. If you can't find it, check your junk folder, or email us and we'll resend it.
